## Design choices for the reviewer

The moment-bin setup follows the reference implementation production form. These are the choices, each as a question.

1. Photoelectric (PE) bin 8-13.6 eV with the Lyman-Werner (LW) band inside it, so LW is part of PE and not a separate non-overlapping bin. The NUV bin carries 6-8 eV. Is overlapping PE and LW acceptable, or do you want disjoint bins?
2. H and H2 ionisation rates both come from the H0 bin. Is that the form you want, or should H2 take its own rate?
3. `HI` holds atomic hydrogen only: no H2 in `rt_kappa`. Is that acceptable for the absorber, or should H2 enter as an opacity hook behind the SGChem flag?
4. `SGCHEM_RT_ABSORBERS_FOLLOW_THE_NETWORK` keeps the network-following variant (HI = x_HI + r x_H2, r = 1.340, band-averaged cross-section ratio; the chemistry consumes H2 at r Gamma) as an opt-in. Keep as an opt-in flag, or drop?
5. Ionising rates reach the network also when G0 = 0 (fix of a line that is the same in the reference, never hit there because it has a background field). Fine as a separate small commit?

## m1/01 Single stars source the moment bins from the tables (114+/18-)
**What and why.** IMF-sampled stars get their luminosity in the M1 bins from the stellar tables. New flag so that M1 builds no longer refuse single stars.
**Evidence before.** Run on the base: the test fails, build refused.
**Change.** `GALSF_SINGLE_STARS_RT_TABLE_BANDS` (default off, needs RT_M1, IMF stars, tables). Sets the base's bins PE, LW, NUV, OPT, generic with the 6 eV split. PE is 8-13.6 eV with LW inside it (design choice 1); NUV carries 6-8 eV. One ionising bin: table photon rate at the bin's mean photon energy. Several: table luminosity per bin. Table loader reads log_Q_ion, log_L_ion_tot (the same two lookups as pi/02) and the four band luminosities.
**Cost.** No field. About 4.5 MB per node of tables. No collective, no global loop; lookups in the existing host source path.
**Evidence after** (measured on an older base; to be re-measured on the audit branch). test/single_stars_rt_bands: energy per bin against tables 1.0009 in all 9 bins (bar 2 %), sum below L_bol, photons in the bins / table Q 1.005 (bar 0.7-1.3), single bin passes. Narrow test. The reference counted LW twice in an earlier form; no number compared.
**Flag off.** See the top of the file.
**Limits.** Stars shine from the end of the pre-main sequence, without the reference's min(t_KH, t_ff) delay of up to 1 Myr (the candidate cause of the earlier HII regions in the 4-seed cloud below).
**Depends on / followed by.** pi/02 lookups overlap (union on rebase). Followed by m1/02.

## m1/10 Start electrons from the chemistry (19+/0-)
**What and why.** With `RT_CHEM_PHOTOION`, the start-up of the moment solver sets every cell fully neutral (HII = 1e-56, Ne = HII) before the first force pass. With SGChem the chemistry has its own start abundances, and the CR fluid divides by the ionised fraction (ion Alfven speed): a start at 1e-56 gives an Alfven speed about 2e5 times too high in the first steps. Here the start takes the chemistry's electrons and absorbers instead.
**Evidence before.** test/sgchem_rt_start (uniform box of test/sgchem_box, 8^3 cells, 2 ranks, IC x(H+) = 0.02): in the snapshot written before any solve, HII and Ne are at the fully neutral start, not at the IC. With the CR fluid (m1/11): Ne 1.3e-56 at the start against 0.020 after the first solve.
**Change.** `sgchem_startup_electrons` (sgchem_cell.cc: electrons per H from the cell's abundances; net 20 takes its H+), called in `rt_set_simple_inits` together with `sgchem_rt_absorbers` under SGChem; the non-SGChem branch is unchanged. 19 source lines.
**Cost.** One abundance evaluation per cell at start-up only. No field, no collective.
**Evidence after.** test/sgchem_rt_start, 2 passed: HII in snapshot 0 equals the IC x(H+), electrons exceed it, and Ne - HII (the C and Si pools) is the same in snapshots 0 and 1 within 1e-9. Narrow test.
**Flag off.** See the top of the file; the change is under SGChem and `RT_CHEM_PHOTOION`.
**Limits.** Fresh starts only; restarts keep their stored state as before.
**Depends on / followed by.** m1/09; followed by m1/11.
