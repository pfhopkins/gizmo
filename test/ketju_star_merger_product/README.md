# ketju_star_merger_product

Table stars colliding inside a KETJU region (`KETJU_MERGE_STARS` with `GALSF_SINGLE_STARS_EVOLUTION_TABLES`, 1 rank, ~100 yr, N-body only).

- `test_product_state`: 5 + 3 Msun head on. The survivor is a main-sequence star of the summed drawn mass, birth Z mass-weighted, main-sequence fraction after Hurley, Pols & Tout 2002 eq. 80, age and wind clock reset.
- `test_contact_radius_from_tables`: a 3 Msun giant and a 1 Msun star with a pericentre between the main-sequence fit and the table radii merge.
