# radp_starside

One 40 Msun star in uniform dusty gas (n_H 300, solar Z, 0.8 Msun cells), production cooling and chemistry (SGChem net 5), 30 kyr, 2 ranks.
Radial gas momentum about the star over the momentum booked in the radiation pressure channel (FeedbackBudget channel 3), bar 0.8-1.1.

Measured: without the fix -0.057 (booked 39.9, delivered -2.3 Msun km/s); with it 0.904 (38.8, 35.1).
Note, the same test without cooling and chemistry (adiabatic gas): -0.068 before, 0.895 after.
