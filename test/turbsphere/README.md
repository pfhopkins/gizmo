# turbsphere

The STARFORGE stirring setup, held at a target Mach number by the `TURB_DRIVING_TARGET_SIGMA` control
loop in its aperture mode: the loop measures only the gas within 2R of the box centre. With
`STARFORGE_GMC_TURBINIT` the aperture `TURB_DRIVING_TARGET_SIGMA=2` is in units of the nominal cloud radius R.

| | |
|-|-|
| units | pc - km/s - Msun |
| geometry | uniform sphere, R = 5 pc, in a periodic box of 10 R = 50 pc; ambient gas at 1e-3 of the cloud density (MakeCloud defaults) |
| resolution | 32752 cloud + 7944 ambient cells of equal mass, on lattices, initially at rest |
| confinement | `STARFORGE_GMC_TURBINIT=2` analytic potential (uniform sphere plus r^-3 halo, scaled by alpha_vir), self-gravity off |
| EOS | `EOS_GAMMA=1.001`, `EOS_ENFORCE_ADIABAT=0.04`: isothermal, c_s = 0.2 km/s |
| driving | solenoidal, parabolic spectrum on wavelengths R/4 - R (Lane et al. 2022, MNRAS 510, 4767), coherence time R/v_rms; lazy force (`TURB_DRIVING_UPDATE_FORCE_ON_TURBUPDATE`) updated every 0.5 = t_cross/10, as turbsphere stirring runs are always made |
| target | Mach 10 inside 2R, i.e. 3D mass-weighted v_rms = 2 km/s |
| mass | 3875 Msun in the cloud: alpha_vir = 5 sigma_3D^2 R / (3 G M) = 2 at Mach 10. This is the sphere inscribed in `test/turbulent_box` |
| variants | hydro / MHD (`MAGNETIC`, `MHD_CONSTRAINED_GRADIENT=1` as in STARFORGE runs, uniform B_z = 2.2 uG everywhere: beta 1 in the cloud, 1e-3 in the ambient, as MakeCloud's field is uniform) |

The ICs are generated by `make_turbsphere_ics.py`. `GravAccel_GMCTurbInit` assumes R = 0.1 BoxSize
and a cloud mass fraction of 0.808 (`STARFORGE_GMC_TURBINIT_CLOUD_RADIUS_IN_BOXSIZE` and
`STARFORGE_GMC_TURBINIT_CLOUD_MASS_FRACTION` in `declarations/allvars.h`); the ICs give 0.805.

**Why the driving stops at R.** Solenoidal modes with wavelengths near the cloud diameter exert a net
torque on the whole cloud, and nothing in the confining potential removes angular momentum: driving
up to 2R leaves a quarter of the kinetic energy in rigid rotation after 25 crossing times (hydro).
The R/4 - R band cuts the torque 3x and the rotation to a few percent over the same time. The band
has ~1.3e5 modes, which the lazy force makes affordable. Driving this small responds within one update,
so the loop's default Kp=2 alternates from update to update; the test sets `TurbDrive_PID_Kp` to 1.

**What is checked.** As `test/turbulent_box` (see `turb_driving_common.py`), with the Mach number
measured over the loop's aperture. The printout also reports the median radius of all gas, as a check
that the cloud is held, but it is not asserted on.
