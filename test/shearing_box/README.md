# shearing_box

Checks the 3D shear-periodic boundaries (`BOX_SHEARING=3`, coordinates (0,1,2) = (r, phi, z)) with MFM, for
q = 3/2 (Keplerian) and q = 1 (flat rotation curve; built with `BOX_SHEARING_Q=(1.0)`). The 2D r-z sheet
(`BOX_SHEARING=1`) is exercised by `test/mri`.

Units: Omega = 1 (the default of `BOX_SHEARING_OMEGA_BOX_CENTER`), L = 1, c_s = 1 (isothermal, `EOS_GAMMA=(1.000001)`), density 1; X = x - L/2, and
eta = v_phi + q Omega X is the velocity relative to the shear flow. ICs are lattices generated by
`make_shearing_box_ics.py`; each q is built once and runs its problems on 4 MPI ranks. The same Config with `MAGNETIC`
added is the MHD variant (below, `test_shearing_box_mhd.py`, `shearing_box_mhd.params`).

| problem | IC | exact solution | checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| equilibrium | 16^3, v_phi = -q Omega X | unchanged | max \|v_r\|, \|eta\|, \|v_z\|, \|drho/rho\| < 1e-6 over 3 orbits |
| epicycle | as above + uniform v_r = 0.3 c_s | v_r = v0 cos(kappa t), eta = -(2-q) v0 sin(kappa t)/kappa, kappa^2 = 2(2-q) Omega^2 | particle-to-particle spread < 1e-6; fitted frequency within 2e-3 of kappa, amplitudes within 5e-3; synchronised final state within 1e-2 v0; > 25% of particles crossed the x-boundary |
| shearing_wave | 32^3 + vortical wave dv = 1e-3, (kx0, ky) = (-4pi, 2pi) | compressible linear shearing wave (Johnson & Gammie 2005), integrated in the test | complex v_r and eta amplitudes within 8% of their peak; linearised potential vorticity i(kx eta - ky v_r) - (2-q) Omega delta conserved to 8% |

Why these are sharp: on a lattice every x-plane slides rigidly with the shear, so all particles in a plane see
identical neighbourhoods and the discrete equilibrium (and the uniform epicycle) is exact up to round-off. The
boundary keeps it exact only if the remap across x = 0/L (phi shift q Omega L t and v_phi offset q Omega L) matches
the interior, so any inconsistency, in the code or in the snapshot writer, shows up as particles leaving the common
solution. The epicycle carries ~5 lattice planes through the boundary each half period. The shearing wave has
ky != 0, so its phase is continuous across x = 0/L only through the time-dependent phi offset, which uniform flows
cannot see. Reversing the phi shift (in the particle remap or in the neighbour search), or dropping the v_phi
offset (in the remap or in the neighbour velocity correction), each fails at least one check by orders of magnitude.

The wave run stops at t = 2.25/q (just after the swing, kx = +ky/4): later, nonlinear harmonics of the wave sheared
to wavelengths of ~4-6 lattice spacings grow exponentially (e-folding ~0.15/Omega, independent of the timestep), a
bulk lattice effect unrelated to the boundaries.


## Cold epicycle (q = 3/2 only)

| problem | IC | checks |
|---|---|---|
| cold_epicycle | uniform epicycle, c_s = 1e-3, v_r0 = 0.1 c_s, dt = 0.077 (MaxSizeTimestep, not Courant); gas for 100 epicycles, collisionless particles for 10 | epicycle energy E = v_r^2 + 2 eta^2/(2-q) (exactly conserved) within 1e-2 at the end (measured: -4.3e-3 gas, -3.3e-5 collisionless) |

The epicycle energy E = v_r^2 + 2 eta^2/(2-q) is conserved exactly, and its cold, Courant-free version checks the time
integration of the Coriolis force: an explicit Coriolis force makes E grow without bound (from the predicted gas
velocity, +80% over 100 epicycles, d ln E/dt = 2.0 Omega^4 dt^3; from the half-kicked velocity of collisionless
particles, ~1e6-fold in 10 epicycles), while the Boris step (a rotation that keeps |v|, taken after all other
accelerations) keeps it bounded at O((kappa dt)^2).

Velocities in intermediate snapshots are half-kicked (written mid-step), which shifts the epicycle phase by
~kappa dt/2; the frequency and amplitudes are fitted, and only the synchronised final snapshot is compared directly.

## MHD variant (`MAGNETIC`)

Ideal MHD in the 3D shear-periodic box (`BOX_SHEARING=3`, q = 3/2, Omega = L = c_s = rho = 1, v_A = B / sqrt(rho)).
B is shear-periodic, so the boundary remaps positions and v_phi but leaves B unchanged.

| case | setup | checks |
|---|---|---|
| windup | uniform B = (B0, 0, B0) in the equilibrium shear flow, 16^3 lattice, 2 orbits | B uniform and flow at equilibrium to 1e-6 (measured ~1e-12); B_phi / B_r = -q Omega t to 1e-6 (measured 1e-13) |
| mri | fastest-growing axisymmetric MRI eigenmode of a vertical field (k = 2 pi / L, k v_A = sqrt(15/16) Omega, s = 0.75 Omega), beta ~ 84, 16^3 and 32^3 | growth rate fitted to rms B_r within 2% of theory at 32^3 (measured -0.5%; -3.0% at 16^3) |

|B| oscillates by ~1e-3 in the wind-up with the kernel volume estimate of the sliding lattice (the density does the same
in the hydro-only `shearing_box` equilibrium), so the wind-up is checked through B_phi / B_r.


Run: `pytest test/shearing_box` (two hydro builds and one MHD build; about 11 minutes on 4 ranks of a Xeon Gold 6244; outputs in
`test/shearing_box/output_<problem>_q<q>/` and `output_mhd_<problem>/`).
