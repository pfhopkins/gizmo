# shearing_box

Checks the 3D shear-periodic boundaries (`BOX_SHEARING=3`, coordinates (0,1,2) = (r, phi, z)) with MFM, for
q = 3/2 (Keplerian) and q = 1 (flat rotation curve; built with `BOX_SHEARING_Q=(1.0)`). The 2D r-z sheet
(`BOX_SHEARING=1`) is exercised by `test/mri`.

Units: Omega = 1 (hardcoded), L = 1, c_s = 1 (isothermal, `EOS_GAMMA=(1.000001)`), density 1; X = x - L/2, and
eta = v_phi + q Omega X is the velocity relative to the shear flow. ICs are lattices generated by
`make_shearing_box_ics.py`; each q is built once and runs its problems on 4 MPI ranks.

| problem | IC | exact solution | checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| equilibrium | 16^3, v_phi = -q Omega X | unchanged | max \|v_r\|, \|eta\|, \|v_z\|, \|drho/rho\| < 1e-6 over 3 orbits |
| epicycle | as above + uniform v_r = 0.3 c_s | v_r = v0 cos(kappa t), eta = -(2-q) v0 sin(kappa t)/kappa, kappa^2 = 2(2-q) Omega^2 | particle-to-particle spread < 1e-6; fitted frequency within 2e-3 of kappa, amplitudes within 5e-3; synchronised final state within 1e-2 v0; > 25% of particles crossed the x-boundary |
| shearing_wave | 32^3 + vortical wave dv = 1e-3, (kx0, ky) = (-4pi, 2pi) | compressible linear shearing wave (Johnson & Gammie 2005), integrated in the test | complex v_r and eta amplitudes within 8% of their peak; linearised potential vorticity i(kx eta - ky v_r) - (2-q) Omega delta conserved to 8% |

Why these are sharp: on a lattice every x-plane slides rigidly with the shear, so all particles in a plane see
identical neighbourhoods and the discrete equilibrium (and the uniform epicycle) is exact up to round-off. The
boundary keeps it exact only if the remap across x = 0/L (phi shift q Omega L t and v_phi offset q Omega L) matches
the interior, so any inconsistency, in the code or in the snapshot writer, shows up as particles leaving the common
solution. The epicycle carries ~5 lattice planes through the boundary each half period. The shearing wave has
ky != 0, so its phase is continuous across x = 0/L only through the time-dependent phi offset, which uniform flows
cannot see. Reversing the phi shift (in the particle remap or in the neighbour search), or dropping the v_phi
offset (in the remap or in the neighbour velocity correction), each fails at least one check by orders of magnitude.

The wave run stops at t = 2.25/q (just after the swing, kx = +ky/4): later, nonlinear harmonics of the wave sheared
to wavelengths of ~4-6 lattice spacings grow exponentially (e-folding ~0.15/Omega, independent of the timestep), a
bulk lattice effect unrelated to the boundaries.

## Cold gas (q = 3/2 only)

Local-disc boxes hold cold gas with shear Mach number M = q Omega L / c_s ~ 30-100. Following Zier & Springel (2022,
MNRAS 517, 2639), two failure modes that the warm lattice problems cannot see:

| problem | IC | checks |
|---|---|---|
| cold_shear | equilibrium shear on a jittered lattice (5% rms displacement), M = 30, 100 at 16^3 and 24^3, 4 orbits | rms velocity noise about the equilibrium (in c_s) not growing in the last orbit (< 1.2x the previous one); noise converging at least first order from 16^3 to 24^3 (**xfail**) |
| cold_epicycle | uniform epicycle, c_s = 1e-3, v_r0 = 0.1 c_s, dt = 0.077 (MaxSizeTimestep, not Courant); gas for 100 epicycles, collisionless particles for 10 | epicycle energy E = v_r^2 + 2 eta^2/(2-q) (exactly conserved) within 1e-2 at the end (**xfail**, both) |

Known failures, marked `xfail(strict=True)` so a fix shows up as XPASS:

- **Shear noise does not converge.** On irregular particles the noise saturates within ~1.5 orbits and stays flat
  (bounded), uniformly through the box, at a level set by the shear Mach number across a particle spacing,
  q Omega dx / c_s = M/N: rms dv/c_s = 0.15, 0.21, 0.37, 0.52, 0.80, 0.86 at M/N = 0.42, 0.63, 1.25, 1.9, 4.2, 6.3
  (rms drho/rho ~ 0.2 at M = 100). It falls more slowly than first order with resolution (0.51 -> 0.37 at M = 30,
  0.87 -> 0.79 at M = 100 from 16^3 to 24^3). At M = 10 it appears late: quiet (~0.02 c_s) for ~2.5 orbits at 16^3
  (~4 at 24^3), then grows with e-folding ~3.7/Omega to 0.21 (0.15) c_s.
- **Epicycle energy grows exponentially.** `GravAccel_ShearingSheet` evaluates the Coriolis term with `VelPred`, which
  the drift has predicted as v_n + dt a_n; the kicks then give v_{n+1} = v_n + dt/2 [F(v_n) + F(v_n + dt F(v_n))],
  Heun's method, whose amplification factor exceeds 1 for a rotation. A model of this exact update reproduces GIZMO's
  growth rate, d ln E/dt = 2.0 Omega^4 dt^3 (9.39e-4 measured vs 9.38e-4 predicted at dt = 0.077; 1.15e-4 vs
  1.14e-4 at dt = 0.038): +80% and +7% over 100 epicycles. Non-gas particles use `P[i].Vel`, the half-kicked velocity
  of the previous step: forward Euler, with E growing ~1e6-fold in 10 epicycles at dt = 0.06. Integrating the
  Coriolis and tidal terms exactly in the kick at fixed position (a rotation of (v_r, eta) at 2 Omega), as in the
  symplectic epicycle integrator of Rein & Tremaine (2011), keeps |dE/E| < 1.5e-3 over 100 epicycles at dt = 0.077
  in the same model.

Velocities in intermediate snapshots are half-kicked (written mid-step), which shifts the epicycle phase by
~kappa dt/2; the frequency and amplitudes are fitted, and only the synchronised final snapshot is compared directly.

Run: `pytest test/shearing_box` (about 5.5 minutes on 4 ranks of a Xeon Gold 6244, including both builds; outputs in `test/shearing_box/output_<problem>_q<q>/`).
