# single_stars_cr_injection

Supernovae and fast winds of IMF-sampled stars with `COSMIC_RAY_FLUID`, on the production path (`MAGNETIC`, `COOLING_OPERATOR_SPLIT`,
diffusion coefficient 1e5 pc km/s). Two boxes: the `single_stars_supernovae` box (core-collapse, failed, PPISN, PISN) and the 35 Msun
wind star of `single_stars_winds_agb_ia`. Bars: ledger rows `E_cr_offered`, `E_cr_injected_sn` = f x the energy of all explosions (1e-3);
gas thermal + kinetic gain = (1 - f) x that (1e-2); `E_cr_injected_wind` = f x the kinetic energy of the wind ejecta (1e-2).
